They Come At Night — Controls

The game is mouse-driven. Click the action buttons at the bottom of the screen
to choose what to do this turn.

  Move           Click the "Move" action, then click an adjacent tile.
  Scavenge       Click "Scavenge tile" while standing on a tile.
  Establish base Click "Establish base here" while standing on a tile.
  Build          At your base tile, click "Open Build menu".
  Settlement     At your base tile, click "Settlement" for the detail screen.
  Inventory      Click "Open Inventory / Assign" to assign items.
  Parley         When an NPC is adjacent, "Talk to <name>" appears.
  Knowledge      Click "Knowledge (N)" to review what your survivors have learned.
  Rest           Heals lead by 1 HP and ends the day.
  End Day        Skip the day's action.
  Save           Save your progress.

Keyboard
  Esc            Cancel current action / open Pause overlay (Pause → Resume / Quit to Menu)
  F12            Take a screenshot (saved to user://screenshots/)

Goal: survive until the megahorde. Kill it and you win.
You always know how many days until the megahorde arrives.
You die if your party is wiped, your lead is killed, or morale hits zero.
