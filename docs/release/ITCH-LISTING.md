## Long Description

### Tagline

The warlord with a heart.

### Overview

WARBAND is a single-player roguelike campaign where you recruit, equip, level, and grieve a warband of named orcs on their climb from farm-raid bandits to warchief of the Northern Crags. Every battle is a 30-60 second auto-resolved pixel-art skirmish. Every orc has a portrait, a name, a voice, and a permanent death waiting to happen.

### Core Loop

**The Tavern (30s):** Three named candidates rotate into view with portrait, archetype, and hire price. A scout report shows the next enemy comp. You have roughly 80% of the gold you need to fill every slot. Commit or pass — passed candidates are gone forever.

**The Brawl (30-60s):** Both sides advance, melee meets in the middle, ranged fires from behind. Big floating text announces kills: "**GROG** crits Bandit for 18!" When your orcs fall, they get a gravestone animation and a place in the memorial. You see exactly why you lost — no hidden RNG.

**Between Battles (5-10min):** Market to sell loot and buy gear. Tavern for fresh candidates. Memorial to read fallen orcs' eulogies. Campaign map to choose your next destination. Survivors gain XP and stat points to allocate.

### Design Philosophy

**Four Pillars:**

1. **Growth Made Flesh** — Every progression beat shows on the orc's body: scars, gear swaps, banner heraldry. Numbers go up only if pixels change too.

2. **Loss Has Weight** — Death is permanent and named. The game gives you reasons to grieve, not just inventory holes. Eulogies matter.

3. **Every Coin a Choice** — Gold is the resource you never have enough of. Recruit OR gear OR heal. Pick.

4. **Watch and Learn** — Combat is auto-resolved but transparent. You can read why you lost and adjust for the next fight. No hidden RNG punishes without teaching.

### Tone

Gallows-humor brutal. Gritty pixel-art in a mud-and-rust palette. Orcs bicker on the way to the fight. The dying ones get last words. The survivors carve names into their armor.

### Current Content (Alpha — G1 Vertical Slice)

Playable: 1 biome, 1 boss, 6 archetypes, 10 enemy types, full Recruit→Battle→Loot cycle. Everything you need to experience the core loop.

### Controls

- **Arrow Keys / WASD:** Navigate menus and campaign map
- **Mouse:** Click to select tavern candidates, inspect units, manage inventory
- **Enter / Space:** Confirm choice, press GO to start battle
- **Esc:** Back / quit menu

### Known Limitations (Alpha)

- **Art is procedural placeholders** — final pixel-art coming at G2 (Public Demo)
- **English only** — localization planned for G4
- **1 biome, 1 boss** — more content being developed weekly
- **Save data may wipe between updates** — alpha means breaking changes possible
- **Balance is rough** — difficulty tuning is ongoing; feedback welcome
