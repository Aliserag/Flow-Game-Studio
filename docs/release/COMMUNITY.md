# WARBAND — Community Playbook

## Welcome

WARBAND is a game about named characters you care about. Our community is the people who care about those characters and each other. This guide sets the tone for how we communicate and collaborate.

---

## Code of Conduct

### Be Civil

- Treat other players, streamers, and the team with basic respect
- Disagreement is fine; insults, slurs, and harassment are not
- No spam, self-promotion, or off-topic flooding

### Be Specific

When reporting bugs or sharing feedback, include:
- What you were doing when the issue occurred
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Screenshots or a description of how to reproduce it

Vague reports ("the game is broken") are hard to act on. Specific reports ("I hired an Archer at level 3 and their attack never fires") are invaluable.

### No Spoilers Without Warning

If you're discussing the story, lore, or late-game boss mechanics, use spoiler tags so others can avoid them.

## Bug Reports

Found a bug? Report it on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/<org>/warband/issues).

### Bug Report Template

Copy and paste this into a new issue:

```markdown
## Bug Title (short & specific)

**Expected Behavior:**
What should have happened?

**Actual Behavior:**
What actually happened?

**Reproduction Steps:**
1. Do this
2. Then this
3. Then this

**Screenshots / Video:**
[Attach if possible]

**System Info:**
- Browser: (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- OS: (Windows, Mac, Linux)
- Game Version: (Alpha G1, exact build date if known)

**Severity:**
- [ ] Game-breaking (can't progress)
- [ ] Major (significantly impacts gameplay)
- [ ] Minor (cosmetic or rare edge case)
```

## Response Times

WARBAND is in alpha. We work on this with best-effort energy:

- **Critical bugs** (game-breaking): Acknowledged within 24 hours, update within 3-5 days
- **Major bugs** (significant impact): Acknowledged within 48 hours, update within 1-2 weeks
- **Feature requests:** Acknowledged, but no timeline promised
- **Balance feedback:** Collected and reviewed bi-weekly

We appreciate your patience as we develop.

## Streaming & Content Creation

You are encouraged to stream, create videos, and make content featuring WARBAND. We ask only that you:

- Include a link to the game in your video description or stream title
- Tag [@warband](https://twitter.com/warband) or use #WARBAND so we can find and share your content
- Respect other players' experiences — no spoiling major boss fights without warning

Content creators are the lifeblood of games like this. We love watching your runs and will amplify the best ones.

---

## What We Won't Tolerate

- Harassment or targeting of other players, streamers, or team members
- Hate speech, slurs, or discrimination based on identity
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic content in discussions
- Discussions of exploits (report them privately instead)
- Demands or entitlement ("you owe me X")

**Enforcement:** First offense = warning + removal of content. Second offense = mute. Third offense = ban.

---

## The Team's Commitment

We commit to:

- Reading and acknowledging your bug reports
- Responding to specific, constructive feedback
- Fixing critical bugs within a reasonable timeframe
- Never making promises we can't keep (no "definitely coming in update X" without certainty)
- Being transparent about delays, cancelled features, and reasons why
- Treating the community with the same respect we ask of you
